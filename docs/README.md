A No-Go Theorem for Phase-Coded Associative Memory under Conservative DynamicsAuthor: Labinot Marku, M.D.Affiliation: KRH Klinikum Nordstadt Hannover, Department of NeurosurgeryDate: December 21, 2025🎯 AbstractThis repository hosts the formalization of a structural impossibility result for phase-coded associative memory systems operating under conservative (gradient-based) dynamics1111. We prove that in any system admitting a Lyapunov energy function and requiring stable, noise-robust retrieval, information encoded exclusively in phase-like degrees of freedom cannot be preserved asymptotically2.The result establishes a sharp boundary between safe pairwise systems ($p=2$) and expressive but unstable higher-order models ($p \ge 3$)3333.🔬 Core TheoremTheorem (No-Go for Phase-Coded Associative Memory):Let $(y, \phi)$ evolve under conservative gradient dynamics with a bounded-below energy function $E(y, \phi)$4. Assume:Flat Fibers: Phase directions are approximately flat5.Isolated Attractors: Attractors are fixed points6.Noise Robustness: Retrieval must be robust to small noise7.Then: No information encoded exclusively in phase variables $\phi$ can be stably retrieved asymptotically8. The system must either collapse phase degrees of freedom (erasing information) or lose fundamental convergence and stability9.📈 Key ImplicationsStructural Failure: The failure of phase-coding schemes is not due to architectural or optimization shortcomings, but is a consequence of geometric and dynamical invariants10101010.The Curvature Constraint: Only directions that generate curvature in the energy landscape can store information under gradient descent11.Universality Boundary:Pairwise Systems ($p=2$): Stable and safe, but cannot support phase-coded memory12.Higher-Order Systems ($p \ge 3$): Can encode richer structure, but lose safety, predictability, and robustness13.🛠 Relation to CONN (Coherence-Oscillator Neural Network)This No-Go Theorem provides the theoretical justification for the CONN architecture. CONN bypasses this impossibility result by:Moving away from "flat" phase-only encoding.Introducing a $\pi$-coherence topological prior to restore necessary curvature to the energy landscape.Utilizing amplitude dynamics to stabilize the manifold.📄 Read the ManuscriptThe full technical manuscript is available in this repository:Download No-Go Theorem (PDF)📋 CitationIf you use this theorem in your theoretical research, please cite:Code snippet@article{marku2025nogo,
